Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: a finished, original iOS app icon for Haku, a Japanese-named app whose theme is peeling a shell open.
Primary request: invent a charming Japanese manga / kawaii chestnut mascot, a peeled-open chestnut (🌰) bursting with personality. Start from a completely new design.
Character design: a plump, rounded, buttery-gold chestnut kernel with a tiny off-center chestnut tip and an irresistibly cheeky, confident little manga face. It is popping up out of its glossy chestnut-brown shell; the shell splits into two rounded cartoon flaps like an oversized open jacket or a little cape. Make the shell opening very clear, like the character has just proudly shrugged it off. A jaunty, slightly tilted pose and lively silhouette. Keep it unmistakably a chestnut, not an onion, flame, acorn, walnut, bird or human. Tiny simple eyes and a playful smile, with a small charming blush accent; no complicated anime eyes. Expressive, cute and memorable rather than babyish.
Art direction: premium Japanese character-goods mascot meets a clean native iPhone app icon. Bold smooth deep cocoa ink outlines, rounded organic shapes, confident graphic curves, thick readable line weight, crisp two-tone cel shading, delightful cartoon proportions. Very few internal details. The shell must not be geometric or sharp like petals. A little graphic comic energy can come from the pose, with at most two compact hand-drawn action ticks next to it.
Palette: replace the cold dark teal direction with an inviting warm cream / pale custard background, rich chestnut and toasted terracotta shell, golden-yellow kernel, deep chocolate linework, and a restrained persimmon-coral blush. A sophisticated small autumn palette, highly legible and cheerful. Color contrast should work when very small.
Composition: one large centered mascot occupying roughly 68-72% of a square canvas, generous balanced safe margins, a simple full-bleed opaque background, no frame. Straight square outer edges; iOS will apply corner masking. 1024 by 1024 composition.
Constraints: no text, no H or other letters, no logo wordmark, no watermark, no grid, no app mockup, no typography, no extra characters or props, no realism, no food texture, no fibrous cracks, no photorealistic highlights, no 3D render, no gradients, no tiny details, no rounded-square border. One finished icon only. Be creative in the mascot silhouette and facial expression while retaining the opened chestnut idea.
